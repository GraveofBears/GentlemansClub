# Changelog

### 1.0.2 (Brought to you by GraveBear)
- Fixed leaving the club sometimes putting you inside the barracks walls. The club now remembers the area you were in when you walked through its door and brings you back there.
- Girls following you now walk in a line in your footsteps instead of piling on top of each other, and move smoothly instead of jittering.
- Ruby, Violet and Mocha now have over a hundred lines each, all their own, with real life stories: a single mum saving for a house, a girl working her way through potion school, a mum of twins saving for a bakery.
- Several hundred new lines for the club-goers and the bouncer, full of Emberville lore.
- The floor pole now stands in front of you when you walk behind it. Violet still dances in front of it.
- Glimmerdust now makes you sparkle.
- The bar now restocks once per in-game day (Bar / RestockHours in the config) instead of every time the club loads.
- Three new songs on the club playlist: Cold Pole, Knees And Toes and Workin' For That Dust.
- A new README, dressed for a night out.

### 1.0.1 (Brought to you by GraveBear)
- Fixed leaving the club putting you inside the walls of another building (most often in a new world).
- You now step out of the club from the doorway's threshold instead of from up against the wall behind it.

### 1.0.0 (Brought to you by GraveBear)
- First release of Emberville After Dark: the Gentleman's Club, with its own interior, stage dancers, club-goers, barkeep, VIP room and bouncer, drinks, Glimmerdust and a music playlist.
- The club is already standing in the starting area on a new install. Debug mode (off by default) adds keys to move or remove it and a coin cheat for testing.
