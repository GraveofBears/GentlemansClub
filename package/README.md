<h2 style="color: #ff4fb8; border-bottom: 2px solid #ff1493; padding-bottom: 5px; text-shadow: 0 0 8px #ff1493;">🌙 WELCOME, STRANGER</h2>
<p style="color: #f3d9ea;"><b style="color: #ffd166;">Emberville After Dark</b> shows you what the town gets up to after sundown: the <b style="color: #ff4fb8;">Gentleman's Club</b>. Walk in through the front door to a neon-lit room with dancers on the poles, girls who chat and gossip, a barkeep with very high prices and a VIP room guarded by a bouncer. <i style="color: #c77dff;">Look, but don't touch.</i></p>
<p style="color: #d8b4e2;"><b>Note:</b> the humour is cheeky and adult (suggestive, never explicit). There's drinking, and a made-up in-game drug called Glimmerdust.</p>

<h2 style="color: #ff4fb8; border-bottom: 2px solid #ff1493; padding-bottom: 5px; text-shadow: 0 0 8px #ff1493;">🍾 ON THE MENU</h2>
<ul style="color: #f3d9ea; list-style-type: none; padding-left: 10px;">
    <li>🪩 <b style="color: #ffd166;">Its own interior:</b> enter through the club's door to a separate room with a stage, a bar, booths, spotlights that sweep and neon that flickers.</li>
    <li>💬 <b style="color: #ffd166;">Club-goers:</b> girls who walk the aisles, with hundreds of lines about life on the pole and in Emberville. Talk to them, tip them, or take a few adventuring. They'll follow you out into the world in a line and complain that you never take them to dinner.</li>
    <li>🍸 <b style="color: #ffd166;">The barkeep:</b> house rules, terrible jokes and a drinks menu that restocks once a day. The <b style="color: #ff4fb8;">Neon Ember</b> cocktail heals a little and gets you drunk. Drink too many and you'll pass out and wake up lighter in the purse.</li>
    <li>💸 <b style="color: #ffd166;">Make it rain:</b> throw coins at a dancer and watch them shower down. Or make it <i>pour</i>.</li>
    <li>🚪 <b style="color: #ffd166;">The VIP room:</b> the bouncer stands in the doorway until you pay him, or bribe him. Then he steps aside, and Ruby gives private dances inside.</li>
    <li>✨ <b style="color: #ffd166;">Trixie:</b> she talks very fast and sells <b style="color: #c77dff;">Glimmerdust</b>. You'll sparkle and run faster for a while, then you crash and you're slower.</li>
    <li>🎶 <b style="color: #ffd166;">Music playlist:</b> plays only inside the club and follows the game's Main and Music volume sliders. Drop your own MP3s into the mod's <code>audio</code> folder to add them to the playlist.</li>
    <li>📝 <b style="color: #ffd166;">Edit what everyone says:</b> all the lines are in <code>text/club_lines.txt</code>. Add a new <code>[girl:Name]</code> section and she'll start turning up at the club.</li>
</ul>

<h2 style="color: #c77dff; border-bottom: 2px solid #9d4edd; padding-bottom: 5px; text-shadow: 0 0 8px #9d4edd;">📜 HOUSE RULES (HOW TO USE)</h2>
<ul style="color: #f3d9ea; list-style-type: none; padding-left: 10px;">
    <li>💋 The club is already standing in the starting area the first time you play. Look for the neon.</li>
    <li>💋 Walk up to anyone in the club and use the game's interact button to talk.</li>
    <li>💋 With <b style="color: #ffd166;">Debug / DebugMode</b> turned on in the config: <b>Ctrl+T</b> moves the club in front of you (in whatever area you're in), <b>Ctrl+Shift+T</b> removes it from the current area, and <b>Ctrl+Shift+G</b> gives you 500 coins. Moves are remembered between sessions, and a removal stays in effect while debug mode is on. Turn debug mode off and the club is back. Step outside the club first.</li>
</ul>

<h2 style="color: #ff4fb8; border-bottom: 2px solid #ff1493; padding-bottom: 5px; text-shadow: 0 0 8px #ff1493;">💎 THE PRICE LIST (CONFIGURATION)</h2>
<p style="color: #d8b4e2;">Run the game once, then edit <code>BepInEx/config/OdinPlus.EmbervilleAfterDark.cfg</code>:</p>
<ul style="color: #f3d9ea; list-style-type: none; padding-left: 10px;">
    <li>🔑 <b style="color: #ffd166;">Debug / DebugMode</b> (false): turns on the keys below. While it's off, the club stays put and the keys do nothing.</li>
    <li>🔑 <b style="color: #ffd166;">Debug / SpawnKey</b> (Ctrl+T), <b style="color: #ffd166;">RemoveKey</b> (Ctrl+Shift+T), <b style="color: #ffd166;">GoldKey</b> (Ctrl+Shift+G) and <b style="color: #ffd166;">GoldAmount</b> (500): a Unity Input System key name, optionally with <code>Ctrl+</code>, <code>Shift+</code> or <code>Alt+</code> in front, or <code>None</code>.</li>
    <li>🍸 <b style="color: #ffd166;">Bar / Stock</b>: what the barkeep sells, as the game's item keys with a min-max amount.</li>
    <li>🍸 <b style="color: #ffd166;">Bar / RestockHours</b> (24): in-game hours between restocks of the bar. What you buy stays sold out until then. 0 restocks every time the club loads.</li>
    <li>💸 <b style="color: #ffd166;">Club / TipSmall</b> (10), <b style="color: #ffd166;">TipBig</b> (50), <b style="color: #ffd166;">MakeItRain</b> (50), <b style="color: #ffd166;">MakeItPour</b> (200), <b style="color: #ffd166;">VipPass</b> (100), <b style="color: #ffd166;">PrivateDance</b> (100): prices in coins.</li>
    <li>🚪 <b style="color: #ffd166;">Club / Bouncer</b> (true): set to false to leave the VIP room open. <b style="color: #ffd166;">ClubGoers</b> (3): how many girls work the floor, 0 to 10.</li>
    <li>🥃 <b style="color: #ffd166;">Drinks / SecondsPerDrink</b> (75): how long each Neon Ember keeps you drunk.</li>
    <li>✨ <b style="color: #ffd166;">Glimmerdust</b>: the price and how long the high and the crash last, plus how much faster or slower you get (in percent).</li>
    <li>🎶 <b style="color: #ffd166;">Music / Volume</b> (0.6), <b style="color: #ffd166;">File</b> (<code>*</code> plays every MP3 in the <code>audio</code> folder; a file name loops just that song; leave it empty for no music), <b style="color: #ffd166;">Shuffle</b> (true).</li>
</ul>

<h2 style="color: #c77dff; border-bottom: 2px solid #9d4edd; padding-bottom: 5px; text-shadow: 0 0 8px #9d4edd;">🎟️ GETTING IN (INSTALLATION)</h2>
<ol style="color: #f3d9ea;">
    <li>Install <b style="color: #ffd166;">BepInEx 5</b> (Mono) for Emberville.</li>
    <li>Place the <b style="color: #ffd166;">EmbervilleAfterDark</b> folder directly into <code>BepInEx/plugins/</code>, keeping its <code>audio</code> and <code>text</code> folders and the <code>gentlemansclub</code> file next to the DLL. If you had the older <b>GentlemansClub</b> version, delete its folder: your settings and the club's spot carry over by themselves.</li>
    <li>Start the game. This mod works on your own game only, so it doesn't need to be installed on anyone else's.</li>
</ol>
